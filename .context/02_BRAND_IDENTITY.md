# Brand Identity

## Core Vibe
GoCar Auto embodies **Trust, Precision, and Premium Service**. The brand operates at the intersection of high-end automotive care and cutting-edge digital convenience.

## Style Rules
1. **Typography & Copywriting**:
   - **Elegant & Concise**: Text copy must be brief, direct, and refined. Avoid technical jargon when speaking to the customer.
   - **Bilingual Support**: Seamless English and Arabic (RTL) typography, maintaining aesthetic balance in both languages.

2. **Visuals & Imagery**:
   - **Photography**: Favor high-fidelity, authentic product and automotive photography over generic vector graphic design fluff. Use real textures, metallic highlights, and moody, well-lit workshop environments.
   - **Aesthetics**: Glassmorphism, smooth micro-animations, and sleek dark/light modes. Ensure gradients are smooth and color palettes are harmonious (curated HSL values).

3. **Tone of Voice**:
   - **For Staff**: Professional, data-driven, and authoritative.
   - **For Customers**: Reassuring, premium, transparent, and welcoming.
