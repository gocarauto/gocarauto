## 2. Business Model Specification (v2.1)

### A. Core Operating Principles
1. **Asset-Light Workshop Concierge**: GoCar operates out of a single central partner workshop facility. The workshop executes physical mechanical repairs, and GoCar coordinates, inspects, delivers, and bills the client with its transparent sliding-scale management fee (10%–15%).
2. **Inbound Phone & Referral Engine**: 100% of new client acquisition is generated through word-of-mouth referrals initiating contact via direct phone calls.
3. **Mandatory Delivery Concierge**: Every service order includes vehicle pick-up and delivery, charged as a dedicated item on every invoice.
4. **VIP Customer Portal as Value Anchor**: Clients have 24/7 self-service access to their vehicle's digital history, upcoming maintenance schedules, downloadable bilingual invoices, and proactive service requests.

### B. New Revenue Stream: Used Car Consignment & Brokerage
- **Mechanism**: Clients or referred vehicle owners request to sell their cars. GoCar visits on-site, inspects the vehicle, captures professional photos, creates listings across Facebook Marketplace and external portals using GoCar contact channels, screens buyers, manages viewings/negotiations, and collects a success commission upon closing the sale.
- **In-Portal Lead Trigger ("Sell My Car")**: Vehicle owners logged into their Customer VIP Portal can trigger a 1-click notification requesting GoCar to handle the sale of any vehicle in their garage, pre-filling known vehicle specs and asking for desired target price.
- **Synergy**: Vehicles serviced with GoCar leverage their immutable digital maintenance history as an authenticated "Service Passport," maximizing buyer confidence and resale value.
