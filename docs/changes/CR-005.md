# Change Request CR-005: Customer Portal Redesign (Slice 1 & 2: Layout, Cockpit, Booking & History)

- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Phase:** Phase 10 (Change Requests)
- **Role:** Builder & Scribe
- **Tooling / Framework:** `pbakaus/impeccable` (Apache-2.0, commit `c3bb418`)

## Objective
Execute Slice 1 & Slice 2 of the customer portal redesign utilizing the newly integrated `impeccable` design toolkit. Eliminate generic AI-slop visual habits (nested card soup, arbitrary glassmorphic halos, jarring elastic bounce animations), and deliver an elite, task-focused automotive concierge interface in **Operate Mode** with native bilingual (Arabic RTL and English) precision.

## Scope of Changes

1. **Third-Party Toolkit & Registry Integration:**
   - Audited and integrated `pbakaus/impeccable` into `.agent/skills/impeccable/`.
   - Recorded provenance in `.valdoz/repos.yaml` and logged architectural decision **DEC-004** in `.valdoz/decisions.md`.
   - Positioned sandboxed engine binary at `.agent/skills/impeccable/scripts/bin/linux-x64/impeccable`.

2. **Customer Portal Shell & Navigation (`Layout.jsx`):**
   - Streamlined sidebar navigation with unified, high-contrast active state indicators and accessible focus rings.
   - Refined bilingual RTL/LTR mirroring with `Plus Jakarta Sans` for English and `Cairo` for Arabic.
   - Polished top navigation bar with streamlined title/subtitle typography, global search bar with keyboard shortcut hint (`⌘K`), theme toggle, language switcher, notifications pill, and primary "Book Service" CTA.
   - Preserved lightweight mobile drawer and unobtrusive direction switcher.

3. **Cockpit Dashboard (`Dashboard.jsx`):**
   - **Fleet Switcher Tab Bar:** Segmented vehicle tabs with live indicators, make/model/plate, and quick link to fleet management.
   - **Hero Vehicle Telemetry:** Replaced nested containers with a high-legibility cockpit layout featuring oil life percentage, brake system health, and odometer strip with tabular numerals.
   - **Live Service Status Stepper:** Integrated multi-stage visual pipeline (`Scheduled` → `Inspection` → `In Progress` → `Quality Check` → `Ready for Pickup`) connecting directly to live customer appointments.
   - **Maintenance Reminders Hub:** Direct alerts linked to pending vehicle service items with one-click scheduling.
   - **VIP Quick Actions:** Streamlined actions for Service Booking, Glovebox, AI Diagnostics Advisor, and Consignment / Sell Car modal.

4. **Service Booking Experience (`ServiceBooking.jsx`):**
   - Engineered progressive 3-step intake wizard (Vehicle & Package Selection → Date & Bay Window Reservation → Technical Notes & Confirmation).
   - Replaced clumsy, oversized cards with structured vehicle selector chips and clear service package summaries.
   - Built arrival window selector with morning, midday, afternoon, and evening slots.
   - Polished confirmation voucher screen with direct actions to dashboard or appointment schedule.

5. **Appointments Schedule (`Appointments.jsx`):**
   - Re-architected appointment card grid with semantic status pills (`Scheduled`, `In Workshop`, `Pending Review`, `Completed`).
   - Highlighted arrival dates and time slots in clean tabular numerals.
   - Added clear vehicle metadata badges and notes callouts.
   - Designed clean empty state guiding users to book new services.

6. **Digital Service Ledger & History (`ServiceHistory.jsx`):**
   - Built a clean, sortable data table with 1px border dividers, subtle hover rows, and tabular numerals for costs and odometer readings.
   - Integrated quick category filters (All, Oil, Brakes, Tires) and real-time search.
   - Added instant one-click actions for viewing online invoice HTML statements and downloading official PDFs.
   - Added summary KPI metrics banner (Total Records, Maintenance Invested, Vehicles Serviced).

7. **Design Quality & Anti-Slop Hygiene:**
   - Ran `impeccable detect` across all `customer-portal/src` components.
   - Replaced dated `animate-bounce` in `AIAdvisor.jsx` with smooth exponential deceleration easing (`animate-pulse`).
   - Added browser surface refinements (themed text selection in `#fbbf24`, custom caret, and smooth scrollbars) in `index.css`.
   - Resulted in **0 anti-patterns and 0 advisories** on `impeccable detect`.
